# Instructions

"This is a part of a solid model of the character chibi Doro from Nikke. It will be integrated into a custom build figurine that will be animatronic. Replace the square socket at the base of the head with a male cylindrical boss projection. Because the final figurine size hasn't been established yet, we'll size the cylindrical projection by percentages of the bounding box size. The axis of the cylindrical projection should align with the centroid axis of the square socket. Let the datum surface for the cylindrical projection be defined by 3 of the surface corner vertices of the original square hole - basically normal to the head in that region. The diameter of the cylindrical projection is 25% of the bounding box width. The height of the cylindrical projection should allow the head to sit flat on it on a table without any other parts touching the table, and should not be more than 12% of the height of the head in this orientation. Don't make any other changes to the head, and ensure the final result is fully watertight and has no hanging vertices, edges, or faces."
